Three steps, start to finish.

No new habits for runners. Chips go out with bibs and come back at the finish.

STEP 1 · PACKET PICKUP

Hand out chips

Each runner gets a chip paired to their bib number. It clips to a shoe or the bib.

STEP 2 · RACE DAY

Cross the mat

The finish mat reads each chip as it passes and records the time automatically.

STEP 3 · AFTER

Collect and reuse

Volunteers collect chips past the line. Results are ready, and the chips are ready for the next event.
